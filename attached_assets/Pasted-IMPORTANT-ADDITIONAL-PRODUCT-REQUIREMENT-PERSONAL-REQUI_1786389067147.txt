IMPORTANT ADDITIONAL PRODUCT REQUIREMENT: PERSONAL REQUIREMENTS AND CURRENT-DEAL INTAKE

The existing application does not collect enough customer information to make reliable personalised comparisons. Add the following functionality while preserving all previous security, research, citation and transaction-boundary requirements.

1. CREATE A GUIDED ONBOARDING AND QUESTIONNAIRE

Add a “My household and requirements” section.

The application must ask structured questions before researching a service. Use a step-by-step wizard with:

* Back, Continue and Save and finish later buttons.
* A visible completion indicator.
* “I don’t know” as a valid answer.
* Conditional questions based on previous answers.
* A final review page.
* The ability to edit answers later.
* Accessible form labels and clear explanations of why important information is requested.

Use versioned, deterministic question schemas in the application for the normal questions. AI may generate targeted follow-up questions when information is missing or contradictory, but it must not replace the core questionnaire or silently change saved answers.

The application must never invent an answer. Save unknown values as unknown.

2. HOUSEHOLD PROFILE

Collect and store, where relevant:

* UK postcode.
* Number of adults and children.
* Homeowner, tenant or landlord.
* Preferred balance between lowest price, service quality and flexibility.
* Willingness to change provider.
* Preference for fixed or variable pricing.
* Existing subscriptions and providers.
* Services the customer may be willing to bundle.
* Any accessibility or communication requirements.

Do not request or store passwords, payment-card numbers, bank details, security answers or supplier-login credentials.

3. SERVICE-SPECIFIC REQUIREMENTS

Electricity and gas:

* Current supplier and tariff if known.
* Electricity-only, gas-only or dual fuel.
* Annual electricity and gas usage in kWh.
* Smart meter availability.
* Electric vehicle ownership.
* EV model if the user wishes to provide it.
* Approximate annual EV mileage.
* Home charger availability and charger type.
* Preferred overnight charging hours.
* Solar panels, home battery or export tariff.
* Fixed or variable tariff preference.
* Green-energy preference.
* Current payment method.

Broadband, television and mobile:

* Current broadband provider.
* Current download speed and desired minimum speed.
* Full-fibre availability if known.
* Number of household users.
* Home working, video calls, streaming or online gaming requirements.
* Landline requirement.
* Existing television subscriptions, including Sky TV.
* Existing mobile contracts, including Sky Mobile.
* Current bundle discounts.
* Willingness to bundle or separate broadband, television and mobile.
* Required television channels or sports packages.
* Contract end date and exit fee.

Car insurance:

* Vehicle make, model and year.
* Approximate annual mileage.
* Personal, commuting or business use.
* Number of drivers.
* Cover level.
* No-claims discount years.
* Voluntary excess preference.
* Required add-ons.
* Renewal date.

Do not collect driving-licence numbers or unnecessarily sensitive identifying information.

Home insurance:

* Buildings, contents or combined cover.
* Property type.
* Owner or tenant.
* Approximate rebuild and contents values where known.
* Valuable items requiring separate cover.
* Cover away from home.
* Claims history supplied by the user.
* Preferred excess.
* Renewal date.

Life insurance:

* Existing cover amount.
* Policy type and remaining term.
* Desired cover and purpose.
* General smoking status only if the user chooses to supply it.
* Whether professional advice is required.

Do not collect detailed medical records. Life-insurance results must be described as preliminary research, not financial or medical advice, and must recommend regulated professional advice where appropriate.

Credit cards and loans:

* Provider and product.
* Current balance.
* Credit limit if relevant.
* Interest rate or APR.
* Promotional rate and expiry date.
* Monthly payment.
* Annual or monthly fees.
* Remaining loan term.
* Early repayment or settlement charges.
* User’s objective, such as reducing interest or paying off debt.

Never collect card numbers, bank account numbers, security codes, login details or security answers. Never apply for credit or initiate a credit search.

4. CURRENT-DEAL RECORD

For every service, add a separate “My current deal” record containing:

* Provider.
* Product or tariff name.
* Monthly and annual cost.
* Usage-based rates where relevant.
* Contract start and end dates.
* Renewal date.
* Minimum term.
* Exit or cancellation fee.
* Included benefits.
* Exclusions.
* Excesses.
* Annual price increases.
* Promotional discounts and their expiry dates.
* Bundle discounts.
* Services included in the bundle.
* Notes.
* Date on which the information was last confirmed.

Each field must record its source:

* Entered by user.
* Extracted from document and awaiting confirmation.
* Extracted from document and confirmed.
* Unknown.

5. DOCUMENT UPLOAD AND EXTRACTION

Add an “Upload my current deal” option accepting PDF, JPG and PNG documents such as:

* Bills.
* Renewal notices.
* Insurance policy schedules.
* Tariff summaries.
* Credit-card statements.
* Loan statements.
* Contract summaries.

Requirements:

* Maximum file size 10 MB.
* Validate the actual MIME type and reject unsupported files.
* Do not make files publicly accessible.
* Do not log document contents.
* Do not permanently store original document bytes.
* Process the file in memory or temporary private storage and delete temporary data after extraction.
* Prefer inline Base64 input rather than retaining files through the Files API.
* Set `store: false` on document-extraction Responses API calls.
* Do not use background mode for document extraction.
* Do not send passwords, payment details or authentication credentials to the model.
* Tell the user that the document will be processed by the OpenAI API before upload.

Use the OpenAI Responses API and Structured Outputs with a strict Zod schema to extract only relevant contract fields. The schema must permit unknown or null values.

Extracted values must be saved as an unconfirmed draft. Present every extracted field on a review screen where the user can:

* Confirm it.
* Correct it.
* Mark it unknown.
* Delete the extraction.

Do not use extracted information for research until the user confirms it.

Structured output does not guarantee factual correctness. The user-confirmation step is mandatory.

6. MISSING-INFORMATION CHECK

Before starting deal research, calculate whether the profile is sufficiently complete for that service.

If important information is missing, block research and display targeted questions such as:

* “Do you have a smart meter?”
* “When would you normally charge your electric car?”
* “Are you willing to move your Sky TV and broadband to different suppliers?”
* “Does your current price include a Sky Mobile discount?”
* “What is your current contract end date?”
* “Please upload a recent bill or enter your annual usage.”

Classify fields as:

* Required before research.
* Recommended for a better comparison.
* Optional.

Allow research with recommended fields missing only after warning that the results may be less accurate. Required fields must be supplied or explicitly marked unknown.

7. RESEARCH CONTEXT AND RESULTS

Send only confirmed profile and current-deal information to the deal-research process.

The research prompt must include:

* Confirmed customer requirements.
* Current deal.
* Existing bundles and subscriptions.
* Required features.
* Renewal date.
* Exit fee.
* Any missing or unknown information.

The comparison must distinguish:

* Headline advertised price.
* Price applicable to this customer.
* One-off fees.
* Expected annual cost.
* Exit fees from the existing contract.
* Bundle discounts gained or lost.
* Price after promotional periods.
* Material differences in cover or service.
* Eligibility conditions.
* Information that still needs verification.

Every results page must show a “Comparison based on” section so the user can see and correct the assumptions used.

Do not describe an offer as the “best deal” unless eligibility, required features and total comparable cost have been checked. Otherwise call it a potentially suitable deal.

8. SAFETY BOUNDARY

The application may:

* Collect requirements.
* Extract and organise contract information.
* Research offers.
* Prepare comparisons and renewal plans.
* Create checklists and reminders.

The application must not:

* Log into supplier, insurer, lender or bank accounts.
* Store supplier-account passwords.
* Accept terms.
* Cancel a contract.
* Switch a supplier.
* Submit an insurance or credit application.
* Run a credit search.
* Make a payment.
* Enter payment information.
* Complete any binding transaction.

All such actions remain with the user.

9. DATABASE AND TESTING

Add appropriate Drizzle/PostgreSQL tables or versioned JSONB schemas for:

* Household profile.
* Service requirements.
* Current deals.
* Field provenance and confirmation status.
* Document extraction metadata without retaining original documents.
* Questionnaire schema version.

Add automated tests covering:

* Conditional EV questions.
* Sky TV and Sky Mobile bundle questions.
* Missing required information blocking research.
* Unknown values not being guessed.
* Document type and size rejection.
* Extracted values remaining unconfirmed.
* Research using confirmed values only.
* Original document bytes not being retained.
* `store: false` being sent on extraction calls.
* The application remaining unable to transact, apply, cancel or make payments.

Do not remove or weaken any requirements from the previous hardening prompt. Before publishing, report the files changed, database migration created, tests run, test results and any remaining limitations.
